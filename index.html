About

The hard part is rarely the pipeline

Five years across financial services, insurance, and consulting has taught me that the difficult part of data engineering is almost never moving the data. It's the schema that drifts at 2am, the lineage nobody can trace, and the governance layer that gets bolted on after the fact.

So I build for those first. Sensitive columns get dropped at an architectural boundary rather than filtered downstream. Schema changes from vendor feeds surface at ingestion rather than as a broken dashboard three days later. Lineage and access control live in one catalog instead of three spreadsheets.

My day-to-day is PySpark, SQL, and Python across Azure and AWS — Synapse and Airflow for orchestration, Databricks and Delta Lake for transformation, Unity Catalog for governance, and Power BI at the reporting layer. I care most about the part where a platform decision quietly removes an entire category of future problem.
